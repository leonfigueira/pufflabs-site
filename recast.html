Recast

Convert almost anything, without uploading a single file.

Get it on the App Store · Free
iPhoneiPad

Pick a photo, video, PDF, audio file, GIF or Live Photo, choose what you want out of it, and Recast does the rest. Every conversion happens on your device. There is no account, no upload, and no waiting on someone else's server.

Name your file size

Most converters give you a quality slider and leave you guessing. Recast lets you say what the file needs to weigh, then works backwards to get there. 25 MB for most email, 20 MB for Discord, 16 MB for a WhatsApp video, or a number you pick yourself.

Honest about results

If full resolution cannot reach your target, Recast lowers the resolution to get there and tells you it did. If a file is already small enough, it leaves it alone and says so.

What it handles

  • Photos: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, BMP, and into PDF
  • PDFs: to JPEG or PNG page by page, or compressed as PDF
  • Video: MP4, MOV and more, with a size target
  • Audio and GIFs, including Live Photos

Private because it never leaves

Everything runs on the device, so it works on a plane and it works with files you would rather not hand to a website. No account and no subscription, and Recast never asks to track you. The only time it goes online is for an advert you choose to watch, and Pro removes that.

Free, with an optional Pro unlock

Recast itself is free: five files on each tool to start, then one a day, and an advert whenever you want one more. Recast Pro removes the adverts and adds unlimited files, whole batches at once, images combined into one PDF, and Shortcuts, as a one time purchase, not a subscription.

Get Recast

Free to download, with Recast Pro as a one-time unlock. No account, no subscription, and your files never leave your device.
