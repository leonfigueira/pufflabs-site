Recast

Convert almost anything, without uploading a single byte.

Get it on the App Store · Free
iPhoneiPad

Pick a photo, video, PDF, audio file, GIF or Live Photo, choose what you want out of it, and Recast does the rest. Every conversion happens on your device. There is no account, no upload, and no waiting on someone else's server.

Name your file size

Most converters give you a quality slider and leave you guessing. Recast lets you say what the file needs to weigh, then works backwards to get there. Under 10 MB for email, under 25 MB, or a number you pick yourself.

Honest when it cannot get there

Sometimes a target simply cannot be reached at full resolution. Recast tells you plainly instead of quietly missing, and offers to shrink the video itself to get closer.

What it handles

  • Photos: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, BMP, and into PDF
  • PDFs: to JPEG or PNG page by page, or compressed as PDF
  • Video: MP4, MOV and more, with a size target
  • Audio and GIFs, including Live Photos

Private because it never leaves

Everything runs on the device, so it works on a plane and it works with files you would rather not hand to a website. No account, no tracking, no subscription.

Free, with an optional Pro unlock

Recast itself is free. Recast Pro removes the adverts and adds unlimited files, batch conversion and PDF merge, as a one time purchase, not a subscription.
