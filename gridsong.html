Gridsong

Tap a grid. Nothing you play can sound wrong.

Built, and with Apple for review
iPhoneMac

Gridsong turns a glowing grid into an instrument anyone can play. Tap a square and it plays a note. Tap a few more and you have a melody, because the whole grid is locked to a musical scale, so nothing you tap can sound wrong.

No lessons, no theory, no wrong notes

Drag across the grid to paint a run of notes, press play, and it loops. Start from a template or a blank grid.

What you get to play with

  • Ten built in instruments including Bell, Pluck, Glass, Voice and Bass
  • Drums, with tempo you control
  • Record your own sounds and play them across the grid
  • Loop, duplicate and layer patterns into a full song
  • Record a video of your loop and share it anywhere
  • Patterns synced across iPhone and Mac with iCloud
