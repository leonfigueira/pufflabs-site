What Merestone is

Merestone puts statutory environmental designations on one map, on your phone, in the field, with or without a signal. The United Kingdom, Ireland, Spain and Portugal, with every layer drawn straight from the agency that publishes it.

  • Drop a pin, choose a radius, and every designation inside it comes back sorted by distance, with anything on site flagged
  • Export a dated PDF with a site plan, every record with its distance, direction and source, and the layers that were checked and found nothing in
  • Download a whole country and keep working with no signal
