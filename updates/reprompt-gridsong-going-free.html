30 August 2026 · Reprompt & Gridsong

Reprompt and Gridsong are going free

Both are moving to a free download, with Pro as a single optional unlock you buy once. Reprompt Pro adds recording, unlimited scripts and importing from PDF, Word and plain text. Gridsong Pro adds the full instrument library, your own recorded sounds and video export. The teleprompter itself, and the grid itself, stay fully usable either way.

Already own Reprompt or Gridsong? Pro comes with it, nothing more to buy.

This is with Apple for review now. I will update this page the day it lands.
