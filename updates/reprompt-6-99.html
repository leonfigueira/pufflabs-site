Nothing about the app changes. It is still one purchase covering iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Watch, with no subscription, no watermark on your video, and no feature held back for a higher tier. Every future update is included, the way it always has been.

If you make videos and have been waiting, this is a good moment.
