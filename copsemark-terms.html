Copsemark terms of use

One payment, no subscription, and the professional judgement stays yours.

Last updated 6 September 2026.

1. Who we are

Copsemark is made by Leon Figueira, trading as Puff Labs, in the United Kingdom. Contact: leonfigueira@gmail.com.

2. What you are buying

The app is free to download. One payment unlocks the finished outputs permanently: the DXF overlay and plan sheet, the PDF plan, the tree schedule, the impact assessment table and the written report, for as many surveys as you like. It is a licence to use the software, not a sale of the software itself.

There is no subscription and no renewal. The purchase is tied to your Apple Account and works on your iPhone, iPad and Mac.

3. The free trial

Before you buy, you can run a 14-day trial that costs nothing. Its length is stated in the app before it starts, along with what stops working when it ends and what the app costs afterwards.

When the trial ends, nothing you have made is taken away. The app keeps opening, every survey keeps opening, you can keep recording and editing trees, the checker keeps working, and you can still export a CSV of your own data. What stops is the finished paperwork: the drawings, the schedule, the impact table and the report. We think a tool that held a consultant's own survey hostage would deserve to fail.

4. When the standard is reissued

BS 5837:2012 is under revision. Rewriting Copsemark for a new edition is a substantial piece of work, so when that edition arrives it will be a separate purchase. What you have already bought is not affected: every survey you have done stays open, and Copsemark keeps producing the 2012 outputs for it, which is what a planning authority working to the 2012 edition asked you for.

5. The judgement is yours

This is the most important clause here, and it is not boilerplate.

Copsemark records what you measure, calculates what follows arithmetically from it, and draws it. It does not survey trees, it does not assess them, and it does not form an opinion. Retention categories, condition, remaining contribution, the effect of a scheme and every recommendation are yours. Where the app cannot know something, it says so and leaves the space for you to write in rather than filling it with a guess.

You are the arboriculturalist. You check the outputs before you issue them, you sign them, and you remain responsible for them and for your own professional indemnity. Nothing the app produces is advice from us, and the app is not a substitute for reading BS 5837 or for the judgement you are paid for.

BS 5837:2012 is published by BSI and is their copyright. Copsemark does not reproduce it and does not replace it. Where the app paraphrases a clause it says so and names the clause, so you can read the source. You need your own access to the standard.

Copsemark draws on those base plans under your licence and prints an acknowledgement on the sheet. It does not re-issue anybody's base drawing as a separate file.
