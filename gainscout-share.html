About this page. These findings are desk checks against official open data, shared from the GainScout app. "No records found" does not prove nothing is present, and a desk check is not an ecological survey. This is guidance for your own research, not ecological certification, a statutory biodiversity metric calculation, or legal, planning, valuation or investment advice. GainScout is not affiliated with or endorsed by any government body. Made by Puff Labs.
